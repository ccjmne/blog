## User guide

This establishment hosts three families of writing.  The [flight
manual](@/flight-manual/_index.md) is the grand assemblage; its individual
articles are the _flight rules_ proper, namely the practical, fairly thorough
guides one may consult with actual intent to improve one's craft.

[Flashcards](@/flashcards/_index.md) are their shorter, more digestible
cousins, each chiefly concerned with lodging one useful notion, pitfall or
habit into memory.  [Ramblings](@/ramblings/_index.md), meanwhile, are where I
permit myself greater altitude and less immediate utility, in pursuit of
perspective, cautionary tales, or the occasional dignified grumble.

This Web site's colour scheme automatically adjusts to your preference as
[indicated by your Web browser](@/flight-manual/web-media-user-preferences.md),
which should forward that of your desktop environment; though it currently looks
better in **light** mode.

Web browsers supporting [View
Transitions](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/View_Transition_API)
will animate, in a non-nauseating manner, various anchor points as you peruse
the site. This functionality may be disabled by indicating, through your browser
again, your preference for reduced motion.

Links like [this one](@/404.md) point to other places on this very blog;
external references such as [that
one](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Attributes/rel#external)
are annotated with an arrow pointing up and to the right.

For the curious ravenous, the section labelled [_Flight
Rules_](@/flight-manual/_index.md) contains the main course.  The
articles there result from a great deal of time, effort, and _learned
experience_, and [are generally nothing alike those you would find elsewhere
online](@/ramblings/on-shoddy-pseudo-tech-guides.md): mine are drenched, coated
and laced in some uniquely overwhelming insight into the greater ecosystem as
a whole, while also not shying away from delving to the very bottom of any
technical well.

For the curious curious, [Flashcards](@/flashcards/_index.md) are their shorter,
more focused, digestible cousins, each chiefly concerned with incepting some
notion into the curious minds.

For the curious contemplative, finally, I also share some
[ramblings](@/ramblings/_index.md) that are more conceptual or less grounded
in relevance and practicality, but may be consumed more recreationally—or
precautionarily.
